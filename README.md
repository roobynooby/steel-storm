# STEEL STORM — 2D Tank Arena

Run me here! :  https://roobynooby.github.io/steel-storm/ 

A complete browser-based, single-player tank game

All CSS, HTML, JavaScript, graphics and audio are contained in `index.html`.

## Controls

- **W / A / S / D** or **arrow keys** — drive your tank
- **Mouse** — aim the cannon
- **Left click** or **Space** — fire (hold to keep firing)
- **P** or **Escape** — pause/resume
- **Touchscreens** — use the joystick and FIRE button; the cannon auto-targets the closest enemy

## Power-ups

- **Energy shield:** prevent damage for 10 seconds
- **Rapid fire:** fire much faster for 10 seconds
- **Repair kit:** gain 35 armor (max 100)
- **Triple shot:** shoot three bullets for 12 seconds
- **Overdrive:** movement speed +45% for 10 seconds
